Make Dallas guest arrivals feel straightforward

An arrival note can do more for your event than another page of attractions.

MEETINGS & EVENTS DALLAS · OCTOBER 6, 2026

Start with the destination, not the airport

Give guests the complete meeting address, the name of the room, and the entrance you want them to use. Add a practical contact for the day itself. If the venue has several entrances, ask it which one best fits your group.

For a multi-day gathering, distinguish the hotel address from the event address. Do not assume those locations are the same.

Give both airports their own instructions

The Dallas area is served by Dallas Fort Worth International Airport and Dallas Love Field. Ask guests which airport they are using before sharing arrival instructions.

Link to the airport or transport operator for current pickup information and transit details. Avoid promising a fixed drive time; flight timing, traffic, and the exact venue all affect the journey.

Offer choices without adding confusion

Visit Dallas provides a transportation overview covering airports and ways to get around. Use that as a reference, then give guests a concise plan that matches your event.

If you arrange a shuttle, state where to meet it, when it leaves, and what to do if a flight is delayed. If guests arrange their own travel, say so clearly. Confirm accessibility needs directly rather than relying on a general description.

Build a useful arrival note

A good note includes the destination, the first required arrival time, parking or pickup instructions, and a contact. Add links for live directions and current operator notices. Keep the essential details at the top.

Send a reminder close to the event after checking that entrances, construction notices, and parking arrangements still match the plan. Give guests one version they can trust.
